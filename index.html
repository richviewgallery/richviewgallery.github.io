Richard, Lead Photographer

Hello, I’m Richard

I am a professional photographer based in Nigeria, the lead photographer at Richview Gallery.

As the Lead Photographer at Richview Gallery, I am passionate about capturing unforgettable moments beautifully and transforming fleeting experiences into timeless visual stories. My photography goes beyond taking pictures; it is about preserving emotions, celebrating connections, and finding beauty in the moments that make life meaningful.

Inspired by real-life experiences, human emotions, and the beauty of everyday moments, my style focuses on creating images that tell stories, evoke feelings, and leave lasting impressions. Whether it is a portrait, a wedding, a family celebration, or a corporate session, my goal is to capture every moment with creativity, intention, and an eye for detail.

To me, photography is the art of freezing time, allowing a single image to tell a story that words may never fully express.

What Inspires My Style

Every frame is guided by a few simple beliefs.

Natural Light

I chase soft, honest light, from morning window glow to golden-hour warmth, so skin tones and moods feel true.

Real Emotion

Candid laughter, a father's proud look, a nervous squeeze of the hand. I photograph feelings, not just faces.

Culture & Colour

Our weddings are rich with fabric, music and tradition. I honour that vibrance with clean, elegant storytelling.
